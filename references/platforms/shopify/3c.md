# 3C / Consumer Electronics Shopify Image Rules

When the task involves 3C / digital consumer electronics (数码) — chargers, power adapters, power banks, USB hubs, docking stations, cables, GaN / PD adapters, wireless chargers, earbuds / headphones / speakers, smartwatches, phone cases, computer peripherals, smart home devices, mini printers, dashcams, e-readers, gimbals, photography lights, etc. — default to this logic. The reference is the **Anker Prime 160W GaN Charger** visual system: dark, illuminated, infographic-driven, museum-level spec communication. Think Apple product page restraint meets Anker's tech-DNA — not Alibaba listing.

## Applicable Categories

- **Charging & Power**: GaN / PD / QC wall chargers, car chargers, wireless chargers, power banks, solar panels, charging stations
- **Cables & Connectors**: USB-C / Lightning / HDMI / DisplayPort / MagSafe / magnetic cables, cable organizers
- **USB Hubs & Docks**: USB-C hubs, Thunderbolt docks, KVM switches, port replicators
- **Audio**: TWS earbuds, over-ear headphones, neckbands, portable speakers, soundbars, conference speakers
- **Wearables & Smart**: Smartwatches, fitness bands, smart rings, smart glasses, AR glasses
- **Phone & Tablet Accessories**: Phone cases, screen protectors, MagSafe accessories, tablet keyboards, stylus pens
- **Computer Peripherals**: Mechanical / wireless keyboards, mice, mousepads, webcams, ring lights, microphones
- **Networking**: Routers, mesh systems, Wi-Fi extenders, 4G/5G CPE, powerline adapters
- **Smart Home**: Smart plugs, smart switches, smart sensors, smart locks, smart lights, hubs
- **Photography & Video**: Gimbals, tripods, ring lights, LED panels, microphones, action cameras
- **Car Electronics**: Dashcams, GPS trackers, car vacuum, car air purifiers, HUDs
- **Office & Productivity**: Label printers, mini printers, document scanners, calculators, shredders
- **Drones & RC**: Mini drones, RC toys, mini helicopters, robot pets
- **E-readers & Handhelds**: E-ink readers, handheld gaming consoles, retro game consoles

## The Core Philosophy

3C products fail in two directions: (1) the white-background marketplace thumbnail that erases all the product's engineering DNA, or (2) the cluttered "feature-bomb" poster that makes the device look cheap. Both are wrong.

**The 3C Shopify image hierarchy is: Power → Specs → Form → Use → Close.**

The viewer must understand **what it does and why it matters** before they appreciate **how it's built** and **how it lives in my life**. The Anker Prime 160W set is a perfect case: the first PDP screen answers "is it small AND powerful?" (the killer combo), the next three prove it (power distribution math, single-port max, smart display), the last one makes it real (sitting on a MacBook).

**Specs are the new beauty.** A $79 GaN charger next to a $30 one is decided by specs and visual proof of specs. Premium 3C images make specs feel inevitable, not argumentative.

## Color Palette Rules

3C palettes are **tech-driven, not brand-driven** — but the Anker Prime system is the gold standard:

- **Premium Dark Tech (Anker Prime / Apple Pro / DJI / Sony)**: Deep black `#0A0A0F` to charcoal `#1A1A22` background. Product hero lit with controlled rim light + accent glow. Electric blue / cyan `#00C8FF` to `#4DA8FF` as the only saturated accent (used for highlights, port callouts, LED display glow). Pure white `#FFFFFF` for headlines and key numbers. Soft grey `#8A8A92` for body copy and footers. **Never introduce red, yellow, or multi-color accents — they scream "sale poster," not "premium tech."**
- **Cool Minimal (Apple / Nothing / Logitech)**: Pure white or warm grey background, product is the entire story. Single soft shadow. No accent color. Number/label in muted grey.
- **Tech Mono (Razer / ASUS ROG / gaming)**: Black + one neon accent (green / pink / cyan) + sharp diagonal lighting. Heavier, more aggressive. Only use when the product category demands it (gaming peripherals, performance hardware).
- **Warm Tech (Dyson / Sonos / Bang & Olufsen)**: Charcoal / warm grey + brass / champagne / copper accent. More lifestyle-leaning, less spec-driven.
- **Lifestyle Pop (Beats / Bose / JBL)**: Bolder color backgrounds (deep red, electric blue, sunset orange) for the hero product. Use only when the product itself is the visual anchor and category is consumer-friendly audio / wearable.

**Universal rule**: One saturated accent color per set maximum. The product's own LED / indicator light is the visual anchor — do not compete with it.

## Default Main Image Logic

Main image recommended 1:1, for search results, collection pages, product card primary.

### Visual Structure

- **Product hero at 55%-70% of frame.** Slightly larger than furniture (where the room is the product) but smaller than fashion (where the garment fills the frame). The product's surface, finish, and form factor must be readable.
- **Background: pure white, deep black, or controlled gradient.** White for catalog cleanliness; black for premium tech hero; gradient (charcoal to deep blue) for brand-led sets. **Never** random lifestyle backgrounds — the product's tech DNA is lost.
- **Lighting: directional, controlled, with rim light + accent glow.** Top-left or top-right key light. Subtle rim light separating product from background. Optional accent glow (cyan / blue / matching product LED) halo around product edges. No flat front-flash. No over-bright HDR.
- **Detail priorities**: Surface finish (matte / glossy / metallic / frosted), key ports / buttons / screen, brand mark, foldable mechanisms (for chargers with prongs), LED indicator.
- **On-image text**: optional. If used, only 1 line — single-word benefit ("Compact" / "160W" / "GaN") in bottom-left or top-left. Never center-aligned. Never specs list.

### Main Image Prompt Framework

```text
Generate a Shopify 3C main image, 1:1 ratio.

Product: {product type, name, color/finish, material, ports/specs, key design details}. Product reference locks exact design — proportions, port layout, screen, brand mark, fold mechanisms, finish.

Background: {pure white #FFFFFF for catalog / deep black #0A0A0F for premium tech hero / dark gradient charcoal to deep blue for brand-led}. Pure flat background, no props, no scene.

Lighting: {Directional key light from top-left, soft rim light on product edges, optional cyan/electric-blue accent glow matching product LED. Surface finish must read clearly — matte vs glossy vs brushed metal. Subtle reflection on surface, no harsh specular.}

Composition: Product occupies 55%-70% of frame, centered or 3/4 view. Brand mark visible but not dominating. Key features (ports / screen / fold mechanism) clearly readable.

On-image copy (optional): Single keyword "{160W / GaN / Compact / Pro}" in small, refined sans-serif (Inter / SF Pro / Montserrat), bottom-left or top-left, white with optional 1px outer glow. No headline, no specs list, no badge.

Style references: Anker Prime product page, Apple Pro product page, DJI Osmo product page, Sony α product page. Premium tech hero, not marketplace thumbnail.

Requirements: Product proportions, color, finish, ports, and brand mark must be accurate. No watermark. No center-aligned sales copy. No cluttered badge stacks.
```

## Default Gallery Image Logic

Gallery recommended 5-7 images. Each image proves ONE spec or use case. The Anker Prime 160W set is a perfect 5-image example.

### Gallery 1: Different Angle / Form Factor

**Goal**: Show the product from a second viewpoint to prove it works from every angle.

- Same product, opposite side, top-down, or 3/4 from a different angle.
- Reveals ports, buttons, screen, fold mechanisms from the back/side.
- White BG or matching dark BG depending on main image choice.
- **No text** unless the angle reveals a key spec that needs labeling.

### Gallery 2: Spec Hero / "One Big Number"

**Goal**: Make the headline spec feel like a fact, not a claim.

- Product as illuminated hero on dark background.
- Headline is the spec: "160W" / "30h Battery" / "40dB ANC" / "100W PD."
- Subhead is the proof: "2× Higher Power Density" / "vs. Previous Generation" / "Lab Tested."
- 3-4 thin-line spec icons at bottom: {icon} {number} {unit} / {label}.
- Footnote in small grey text: "Based on internal testing..." / "Compared to previous model..."

This is the **Anker Prime "AirPods-Size 160W Power" screen** — and it's a templated pattern for any spec-driven 3C product.

### Gallery 3: Real-Device Comparison / Use Case Proof

**Goal**: Make the spec believable by showing the product in action with real devices.

- Product shown next to the devices it powers / pairs with / improves.
- Examples: charger next to MacBook + iPhone + iPad, earbuds next to phone showing case, keyboard next to monitor + mouse, hub next to laptop with multiple cables plugged in.
- Optional math/equation overlay if comparing replacement value: "160W = 140W + 35W + 35W" or "3 Devices = 1 Charger."
- Headline is the use case: "Next-Gen Dynamic Power Distribution" / "Charge 3 Devices Simultaneously" / "One Hub, 12 Ports."

This is the **Anker Prime "Next-Gen Dynamic Power Distribution" screen** — the gold standard for charger / hub / dock products.

### Gallery 4: Port / Interface Callout

**Goal**: Show technical capability with line connector annotations.

- Product close-up or full product with callout lines pointing to key interfaces.
- Each callout: thin line + small icon + spec number ("C1: 140W Max" / "HDMI 2.1" / "USB4 40Gbps").
- Optional hand or cable interacting to add scale and "in-use" energy.
- Background: dark gradient or matching main BG.

This is the **Anker Prime "Enjoy 140W Max from Any Single Port" screen** — copy the pattern for any port-rich product (hubs, docks, multi-charger stations, audio interfaces).

### Gallery 5: UI / Smart Feature (Optional, for smart products)

**Goal**: Show the screen / app / smart features that differentiate the product.

- Black BG with floating UI cards radiating from the central product.
- Each card is a feature: display modes, charging modes, app screens, smart protection, Bluetooth pairing, etc.
- Top: 3-4 key metrics with big numbers ("8 Display Features / 7 Charging Modes / 0.3s Screen Switch").
- Center: product with screen turned on showing real-time data.
- This is the **Anker Prime "Smart Touch Display and Seamless Control" screen** — copy for any product with a screen, app, or smart feature.

If the product has no UI / screen / app, skip this and replace with another spec callout or in-use scene.

### Gallery 6: Size / Portability Proof (Optional)

**Goal**: Prove compactness with size comparison.

- Product placed next to a familiar everyday object (MacBook, iPhone, AirPods case, palm, keychain, coffee cup).
- Optional dimension lines or in-frame dimensions text.
- Headline: "Super Compact and Travel-Friendly" / "Pocket-Sized" / "Fits in Any Bag."
- Dark BG with the comparison object lit cleanly.

This is the **Anker Prime "Super Compact and Travel-Friendly" screen** — copy for any portable 3C (chargers, power banks, earbuds, mini speakers, gimbals).

### Gallery 7: Variants / Color Options

**Goal**: Show all colorways as a unified system.

- All variants in same angle, same lighting, same background. Only color changes.
- Color name beneath each: "Starlight Silver / Midnight Black / Sage Green."
- Use clean 3-4 grid layout. No lifestyle scenes per variant — that's PDP work.
- For chargers / cables / hubs: typically 1-2 colors only. For audio / wearables: 3-5 colors common.

## Default PDP Logic

3C PDP is a **spec narrative**, not a room walkthrough. Each screen answers a specific buyer question in order. The Anker Prime 160W PDP is a 5-screen masterpiece — copy the structure for any 3C product.

### 5-7 Screen PDP Structure (Anker Prime Model)

1. **Hero Spec / The Killer Combo**: One big number + one big comparison. "AirPods-Size 160W Power" = solves "is it small AND powerful?" simultaneously. Optional: brand badge (No.1 / Award / Patent) and CES-style third-party credibility mark.
2. **Use Case Proof / Real-Device Scenario**: Show the product doing the work. "Next-Gen Dynamic Power Distribution" = 3 devices, 1 charger, clear math. Use case + spec together.
3. **Capability Deep-Dive / Port Callout**: Drill into the technical detail that matters. "140W Max from Any Single Port" = every port is full power, hand+cable makes it tangible.
4. **Smart Feature / UI**: Show the screen / app / modes that create the premium experience. "Smart Touch Display" = 8 features + 7 modes + floating UI cards.
5. **Size / Lifestyle Proof**: Prove the form factor in a real setting. "Super Compact and Travel-Friendly" = sits on a MacBook trackpad, sized against AirPods.
6. **Specifications / Specs Sheet** (optional, for spec-driven 3C): Clean spec list with categories (Output / Input / Compatibility / Dimensions / Weight). Architectural-drawing style typography.
7. **In the Box / Ecosystem / Close**: What's included + ecosystem compatibility + purchase trigger. "In the box: charger, manual, 18-month warranty."

### PDP Coherence Rules (3C Edition)

- **Unified background tone across all screens.** The Anker Prime set is dark gradient → black → dark → black → dark. Pick ONE dark family and commit. No screen should suddenly look like a white-background catalog.
- **Unified accent color.** Pick the product's own LED / brand accent (Anker = cyan, Nothing = white-on-red, Apple = no accent, DJI = red) and use it consistently for highlights, callouts, port labels.
- **Unified typography family.** One sans-serif family throughout. Inter / SF Pro / Roboto / Montserrat. Title in Bold/Black, body in Regular. Numbers can be in mono if specs are central.
- **Unified product anchor.** Product appears in every screen (or almost every screen), at consistent scale, consistent finish, consistent color. No variant drift.
- **Unified info-card style.** If Screen 3 has round-corner cards with thin line icons, Screen 4's cards use the same radius, same icon style, same callout line weight.
- **Unified footnote style.** Every spec-bearing screen has a small grey footnote at bottom. Same position, same typography, same voice ("Based on internal testing..." / "Compared to previous generation...").
- **Same light direction.** If product has top-left key light in screen 1, the hand+cable close-up in screen 3, the floating UI in screen 4, and the size comparison in screen 5 all use top-left key light.

## Variant Image Logic

3C variants are color / configuration stories:

- **Color variants** (audio, wearables, phone cases): 1 white-BG product shot + 1 dark-BG lifestyle shot per color. White BG shows accurate color, dark BG shows finish under premium light.
- **Capacity variants** (power banks, storage): 1 white-BG product shot + 1 comparison shot showing all capacities side by side with size/dimensions labeled.
- **Configuration variants** (hub port counts, charger wattages): Single image showing all configurations in a row with callout labels. e.g. "5-port / 7-port / 12-port."

Color names must be specific: "Midnight Black = matte deep charcoal with subtle blue undertone" / "Sage Green = desaturated muted green-grey" / "Champagne Gold = warm metallic gold with brushed finish."

## Promotional Banner Logic

3C promo banners can be more dynamic than other categories — but still need to look premium, not Amazon-deal:

- **Single product hero + single benefit + seasonal context.** e.g. "Black Friday: $20 Off" / "New Arrival: Prime 160W" / "Holiday Gift Guide."
- **Color stays in the product's own palette.** A red "SALE" badge over an Anker Prime cyan/black set is visual violence.
- **One accent color, one headline, one product, one number.** That's it.
- **Can use diagonal / slanted layout** for sale banners (slightly more energetic) but keep type and product rendering premium.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Pure white BG for everything.** 3C products with rich finishes (brushed metal, glass, LED screens) lose 80% of their appeal on pure white. Use dark BG for premium tech, white BG only for catalog / comparison shots.
2. **Spec-dump infographic walls.** A single screen with 17 specs in 11 icons looks like a spec sheet, not a premium product page. Spread specs across screens; one screen, one key message.
3. **Decorative fake UI elements.** Floating "Bluetooth Connected" or "AI Mode 2.0" callouts that don't correspond to the actual product are a credibility killer. Every UI element shown must be a real feature the product has.
4. **Wrong color accent.** Apple products don't get red callouts. Premium audio doesn't get neon green highlights. The accent color must match the product's own LED / brand mark.
5. **Inconsistent product appearance across the set.** The product in Screen 1 looks slightly different from the product in Screen 5 (port color shifted, finish different, brand mark moved). Lock the product appearance in the very first prompt and never drift.
6. **Staged lifestyle scenes.** A charger floating in a coffee shop next to a latte is not a 3C image — it's a stock photo. The product belongs in its real use case (next to devices, on a desk, in a tech bag), not in a curated lifestyle scene.
7. **No footnote / spec source.** "160W" with no footnote looks like a marketing claim. "160W Max. Based on internal testing in AI Mode 2.0." with a small grey footnote looks like engineering credibility. Always add footnotes.
8. **Cluttered brand badges.** Stacking "Award Winner! / Best Seller! / Patent Pending! / 5-Star Reviews!" is a marketplace move. One or two strategic badges (e.g. CES Innovation Awards 2026, The World's No.1 Mobile Charging Brand) is enough. Pick credibility over quantity.

## Anker Prime 160W — Visual System Reference Card

For any 3C product asking "what does premium tech PDP look like?", here is the locked Anker Prime 160W visual system that should be carried into similar products:

```markdown
- Background family: Dark gradient #0A0A0F → #1A1A22 → pure black, with optional radial dot pattern (light tunnel / X-ray effect) on hero screens
- Accent color: Electric blue / cyan #00C8FF — used for LED display glow, port callout lines, spec numbers, "PowerIQ" label
- Typography: Inter or SF Pro — Bold for headlines, Regular for subhead, optional mono for spec numbers
- Product anchor: Charger always centered, consistent 3/4 angle or front view, foldable prongs visible from left side
- Info cards: Rounded corners (8-12px radius), semi-transparent dark fill, thin white outline, line icon + number + label
- Callout lines: 1px white lines with 90° bend, ending in small dot at port
- Footnote style: 8-10px grey text, bottom-center, "Based on internal testing..." voice
- Brand badges: Top-left corner only, single high-credibility badge (No.1 / CES Innovation Awards), no badge stacking
- Light direction: Top-left key, subtle rim light, accent glow around product edges
- Hero spec format: "[Familiar-Object]-Size [BIG NUMBER] [Unit]" = "AirPods-Size 160W Power"
- Math format: "[Total] = [A] + [B] + [C]" with equivalent claim underneath
```

## One-Line Summary

3C imagery is **spec storytelling**: the headline number must feel like a fact, the comparison must be undeniable, the product must look like engineering made physical. **Specs are the new beauty, dark backgrounds are the new white, and footnotes are the new credibility.**
